Protect your privacy before sharing.

Sharing screenshots or photos online can accidentally reveal sensitive data like passwords, addresses, credit card numbers, or children's faces. BlurDiet allows you to selectively hide these details instantly.

Redaction Modes

  • ✨ Auto-Face Blur: Uses AI to automatically detect and blur all faces in the photo instantly.
  • 💧 Blur: Softens details. Best for hiding background clutter while keeping the image looking natural.
  • 👾 Pixelate: The classic "Censored" look. Best for hiding text, numbers, or license plates.
  • ⬛ Blackout: Draws a solid black box. Use this for maximum security when hiding highly sensitive data like passwords.

Security Promise

Can the blur be reversed?

No. Once you download the image, the blurred pixels are "baked in." The original data underneath is destroyed and replaced by the blur effect.

Where is the image processed?

100% in your browser. We never upload your sensitive screenshots to any server.
