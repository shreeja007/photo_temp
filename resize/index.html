Frequently Asked Questions

Does resizing reduce quality?

Making an image smaller (downscaling) usually keeps it sharp. Making it larger (upscaling) may cause blurriness, but SizeDiet uses smart smoothing to help.

How does "Lock Aspect Ratio" work?

When the lock is on (🔒), changing the width automatically adjusts the height to keep your image from looking squished or stretched.
