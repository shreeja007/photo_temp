About PhotoDiet

PhotoDiet was built to solve a simple problem: Optimizing images shouldn't require uploading your private data to a random server.

Most online tools upload your files to their cloud, process them, and send them back. This is slow and a privacy risk. PhotoDiet uses modern WebAssembly and Canvas API technology to process everything directly on your device. It's faster, safer, and completely free.
