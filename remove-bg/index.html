Why use ClearDiet Background Remover?

ClearDiet uses advanced AI running directly in your browser to instantly remove backgrounds from images. Unlike other tools, your photos never leave your device, ensuring 100% privacy.

Key Features

  • 🤖 AI Auto-Removal: Instantly detect and isolate subjects with one click.
  • 🖌️ Manual Eraser & Restore: Fine-tune edges with professional brush tools (Eraser/Restore modes).
  • 🔒 Privacy First: Zero server uploads. Works completely offline after loading.
  • ✨ High Quality: Exports transparent PNGs at full original resolution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClearDiet free?

Yes, ClearDiet is completely free to use with no daily limits, no sign-ups, and no watermarks.

Do my photos get uploaded to a server?

No. We use WebAssembly technology to run the AI model strictly on your device. Your images never leave your browser tab.

What is "Restore Mode"?

If the AI accidentally removes part of your subject, switch to Restore Mode to paint those pixels back in using the original image data.
