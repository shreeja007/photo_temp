Why remove EXIF metadata?

Most modern smartphones and cameras automatically embed hidden data (EXIF) into every photo you take. This can include the exact GPS coordinates of your home, the date the photo was taken, and your device model.

What DataDiet Removes:

  • Geolocation: Latitude and longitude coordinates that pinpoint where the photo was taken.
  • Device Info: Phone model, lens type, and software version.
  • Settings: Shutter speed, ISO, aperture, and flash settings.
  • Timestamps: Exact date and time of capture.

Common Use Cases

  • Selling items online: Don't reveal your home address in photos of items for sale.
  • Social Media: Protect your privacy when posting on public forums (Reddit, Twitter).
  • Whistleblowing / Journalism: Ensure total anonymity by scrubbing source device data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this affect image quality?

DataDiet creates a new copy of your image. While it preserves visual quality (set to 100%), technically it is a new file. The visual difference is imperceptible.

Can I choose to keep some data?

No. For maximum security, DataDiet uses a "nuclear" approach. It completely destroys the metadata container so nothing is accidentally left behind.
