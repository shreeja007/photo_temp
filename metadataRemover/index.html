Why remove EXIF metadata?

Most modern smartphones embed hidden data (EXIF) into every photo. This often includes GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude), the exact date/time, and your device model.

What DataDiet Removes:

  • Geolocation: Exact map coordinates of where the photo was taken.
  • Device Info: Phone model, lens type, and software version.
  • Settings: Shutter speed, ISO, aperture, and flash settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this affect image quality?

DataDiet creates a new copy of your image. While it preserves visual quality (set to 100%), technically it is a new file container, which guarantees the old metadata is gone.

Is it safe?

Yes. The image processing happens in your browser. Note: If your photo has GPS data, we send only the coordinates (not the photo) to OpenStreetMap to show you the address address preview above.
